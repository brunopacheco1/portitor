# Spike: the DPV EHDS purposes and EHDS Article 53(1)

Date: 2026-11-28. A research note for
[R-40](../RISKS_AND_TECHNICAL_DEBTS.md#r-40), docs only. Every source
below was read on 2026-11-28.

## The question

Since 1.4 an offer may state a `purpose` as any IRI
([ADR 0009](../adr/0009-minimal-odrl-policies.md), amendment in 1.4).
The docs and the health scenarios use purposes of the Data Privacy
Vocabulary's EHDS extension (DPV EHDS). The 1.4 amendment says DPV
"does not map them to the points of Article 53". Is that so, which DPV
purpose goes with which point of Article 53(1), and are the IRIs we use
right?

**Result:** DPV EHDS 2.3 does name the point of Article 53(1) for each
of its secondary use purposes, in a `dct:source` text on each term.
Every point of the adopted Article 53(1), (a) to (f) with (e)(i) and
(e)(ii), has at least one DPV purpose. The link is a plain string, not
a machine-readable relation, and the extension is still a draft. The
two IRIs Portitor's docs and scenarios use exist and are used for the
right points. The 1.4 sentence was wrong for DPV 2.3.

## What DPV EHDS is

- Namespace `https://w3id.org/dpv/legal/eu/ehds#`, prefix `eu-ehds`.
- Version 2.3 (`owl:versionInfo`), modified 2026-02-25,
  `bibo:status` draft. The page says "Draft Community Group Report 25
  February 2026". A Community Group report is not a W3C standard.
- 26 purposes, each `a rdfs:Class, skos:Concept, dpv:Purpose`, each
  with a `skos:broader` term of the same name in the DPV health sector
  extension (`https://w3id.org/dpv/sector/health#`).
- The only link to the law is `dct:source`, a string such as
  `"EHDS Art.53.1(e)"`. There is no `dpv:hasLegalBasis` and no
  `rdfs:seeAlso`.
- Versions 2.1 and 2.2 cited the proposal's numbering (`EHDS Art.34.1`,
  points (a) to (h)). Version 2.3 cites the adopted Article 53(1). It
  dropped two terms only the proposal supported
  (`DevelopSocialSecurityProductsAndServices`, `PersonalisedHealthcare`;
  marked `deprecated` in the source spreadsheet, "not in EHDS, moved to
  health sector"). Version 2.4-dev has the same 26 purposes and the same
  sources.

## Article 53(1) and the DPV purposes

Article 53(1), chapeau: "Health data access bodies shall only grant
access to electronic health data referred to in Article 51 for
secondary use to a health data user where the processing of the data by
that health data user is necessary for one of the following purposes".
The adopted text has points (a) to (f); there is no (g) or (h). Article
53(2) reserves (a), (b) and (c) for public sector bodies and Union
institutions, bodies, offices and agencies.

"Explicit" below means the DPV term's `dct:source` names that point.
The IRIs are `https://w3id.org/dpv/legal/eu/ehds#` plus the name.

| Art. 53(1) | The Regulation (short quote) | DPV EHDS 2.3 purpose | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (a) | "the public interest in the areas of public or occupational health, such as activities to protect against serious cross-border threats to health, public health surveillance or activities ensuring high levels of quality and safety of healthcare ... and of medicinal products or medical devices" | `PublicInterestRelatedToHealth`; for the examples: `ProtectAgainstCrossBorderThreatsToHealth`, `PublicHealthSurveillance`, `EnsureQualitySafetyHealthcare`, `EnsureQualitySafetyMedicinalProducts`, `EnsureQualitySafetyMedicalDevices` | Explicit. The five example terms are not `skos:broader` of the first; all six cite (a) |
| (b) | "policymaking and regulatory activities to support public sector bodies or Union institutions ..." | `PublicPolicyMaking`, `SupportAuthoritiesRegulatoryActivities` | Explicit |
| (c) | "statistics as defined in Article 3, point (1), of Regulation (EC) No 223/2009 ..." | `ProvideHealthcareOfficialStatistics` | Explicit |
| (d) | "education or teaching activities in health or care sectors at vocational or higher education level" | `ImproveHealthcareEducation` | Explicit. The definition ("improving the provision of healthcare education") leaves out "vocational or higher education level" |
| (e) | "scientific research related to health or care sectors that contributes to public health or health technology assessments, or ensures high levels of quality and safety ... with the aim of benefiting end-users" | `HealthcareScientificResearch` | Explicit. The definition ("healthcare scientific research") leaves out the conditions of (e) |
| (e)(i) | "development and innovation activities for products or services" | `DevelopPublicHealthProductsAndServices` | Explicit. Its definition adds "that contribute to public health", wording of the proposal's point (f) |
| (e)(ii) | "training, testing and evaluation of algorithms, including in medical devices, in vitro diagnostic medical devices, AI systems and digital health applications" | `TrainTestAndEvaluateHealthAlgorithms`; `TrainTestAndEvaluateMedicalDevicesAlgorithms`, `TrainTestAndEvaluateAISystemsAlgorithms`, `TrainTestAndEvaluateHealthAppsAlgorithms` | Explicit. No term for in vitro diagnostic medical devices |
| (f) | "improvement of the delivery of care, of the optimisation of treatment and of the provision of healthcare, based on the electronic health data of other natural persons" | `ImproveHealthcare` | Explicit (new in 2.3) |

Nine more DPV EHDS purposes cite Article 2(2)(d), the definition of
primary use, not Article 53(1): `MedicinalProductsPrescription`,
`MedicalDevicesPrescription`, `MedicinalProductsDispensation`,
`MedicalDevicesDispensation`, `MedicinalProductsProvision`,
`MedicalDevicesProvision`, `SocialServicesProvision`,
`AdministrativeServicesProvision`, `ReimbursementServicesProvision`.
They are not purposes of secondary use; an offer for secondary use
should not name them.

## Gaps

- **Draft.** DPV EHDS is a draft Community Group report. Terms were
  added and removed between 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3. An IRI we use today can
  be deprecated tomorrow.
- **A text, not a relation.** The point of Article 53(1) is a free-text
  `dct:source`. A program cannot ask "which purposes belong to (e)"
  without parsing that text.
- **No hierarchy for a point.** The terms for (a) and (e)(ii) are not
  linked to each other by `skos:broader`; each has a broader term in the
  health sector extension instead. So a permit for (a) does not "contain"
  `PublicHealthSurveillance` in any machine-readable way. Portitor
  compares purposes exactly anyway (ADR 0009).
- **Definitions shorter than the law.** The conditions of (d) and (e)
  are not in the DPV definitions. The legal meaning comes from the
  Regulation and the data permit, not from the IRI.
- **Not reused by HealthDCAT-AP.** Release 8 ("European Commission
  Draft Specification") has `dpv:hasPurpose` on a dataset, range
  `dpv:Purpose`, with no controlled vocabulary. Its examples use blank
  nodes with a free-text description. The repository has no
  `dpv/legal` or `eu-ehds` IRI. So the bundle (HealthDCAT-AP shapes)
  does not check which purpose IRIs a dataset uses.
- **No EHDS permit template yet.** The data permit's purpose field has
  no adopted identifier list (ROADMAP: the permit scenario waits for
  the template, 1.10).

## The IRIs Portitor uses

Searched in the repository on 2026-11-28 (`grep` for `dpv/legal/eu/ehds`
and `eu-ehds:`). Only two IRIs are used. Both exist, exactly as written,
in DPV EHDS 2.3 and 2.4-dev.

| IRI | Where | Used for | Right? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `eu-ehds:HealthcareScientificResearch` | `scenarios/hdab-intermediary` (the lab's offer), `scenarios/data-permits` (offers and the permit's `purpose` claim), DEPLOYING, ADR 0009, a harvest test and policy tests | Research on genotypes by a university lab | Yes: Art. 53(1)(e). The data-permits README already says (e) |
| `eu-ehds:ImproveHealthcareEducation` | `scenarios/hdab-intermediary` (step 1, the refused request) | "Another purpose" that must be refused | Yes as an example: Art. 53(1)(d). Any other purpose would do |

No change to the scenarios is needed.

## What this means for Portitor

- Keep accepting any IRI and comparing exactly (ADR 0009). Shipping a
  list would tie Portitor to a draft.
- The docs may name DPV EHDS 2.3 as the source of example purposes, with
  the point of Article 53(1) next to each, and say it is a draft.
- The HDAB, which issues the data permit, is the party that knows the
  purpose in law. A future permit credential could carry both the DPV
  IRI and the point of Article 53(1); that waits for the EHDS permit
  template (1.10).
- R-40 stays Open: a mapping exists now, but in a draft, and neither
  HealthDCAT-AP nor an EHDS permit template uses it.
